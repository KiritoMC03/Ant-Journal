# 2026-10-25 · Acanthomyrmex glabfemoralis · ag-01

[Archive](../../Archive.md) · [Colony card](../../Colonies/ag-01.md)


## Observations

This was the first time I conducted a thorough observation for several minutes.
The ants are surprisingly bold and active (which differs from the experiences of some other keepers).
They aren't afraid of the cotton ball moving in the test tube, aren't afraid of the cotton swab when I remove waste, and weren't afraid to explore the foil with syrup.
There was no sign of anxiety or frantic activity among the ants; rather, they seemed to be curiously exploring what was happening around them.
The soldiers tend to stay close to the cotton ball and don’t run off to explore everything, unlike the workers.
