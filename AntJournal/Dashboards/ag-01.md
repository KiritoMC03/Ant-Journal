## Legend

Points use observation filename dates. Missing days are not zeros. Lines and interpolation are disabled. Tracker uses numeric projections; the table and source fields retain ~approximation. ? creates no point. An empty journal can produce Tracker's "No valid date" message; the core block explains the empty state.
