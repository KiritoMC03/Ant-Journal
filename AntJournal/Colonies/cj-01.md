# Camponotus japonicus · cj-01

## Permanent information

These ants are quite timid and cautious. They react very strongly to disturbances.
The queen arrived missing half of her right front segment and occasionally snores as she walks.
In terms of protein, they prefer *Drosophila hydei* to beetle larvae (at least, I haven't seen any evidence to the contrary yet).
They are extremely neat (yet).
