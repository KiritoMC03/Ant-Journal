## What people may do

Human use is royalty-free, including reading, study, execution, modification, translation, commercial use, sale, redistribution, and forking. Ordinary tools remain permitted. Private changes need not be published.

The grant has two essential conditions: preserve the license/notices, and preserve the LLM restriction in distributed copies and adaptations. Thus it is not literally an unconditional grant for every use. Those conditions are necessary for the intended restriction to follow copies and forks. It is a custom source-available license, not an MIT, CC0, or OSI-approved open-source license. The [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd) requires freedom across fields of endeavor and technology neutrality.

## What the license prohibits

Without separate permission from the relevant copyright holders, covered material cannot be supplied to an LLM or multimodal language model for reading, analysis, summaries, questions, code review, editing, inference, retrieval, embeddings for an LLM, training, fine-tuning, distillation, evaluation, or dataset creation. The rule includes local models and AI coding agents, even with a person approving output.

The restriction follows inherited material and adaptations in forks, forks of forks, mirrors, releases, and other distributions. Renaming or relicensing a fork cannot remove it. Independent works merely distributed alongside the material and separately licensed dependencies are outside that scope. A maintainer can authorize only the rights that maintainer actually controls; permission from a fork owner alone does not waive another holder's rights. Specific maintenance permission is not training permission.

## Legal and technical limits

A license cannot guarantee that nobody will fetch a public repository or feed a copy to a model. It provides no authentication, network block, or automatic enforcement. Copyright does not protect every fact, method, or AI-generated output; restrictions can apply only where the relevant rights exist. Mandatory exceptions, including any applicable fair-use or research/mining exceptions, remain governed by law. Earlier separate grants cannot be revoked through this notice. Enforceability depends on the work, jurisdiction, access, and applicable agreements; this custom text has not been independently reviewed by counsel.

For AI-related copyright questions, distinguish copyrightability of outputs from the legality of model training; the [U.S. Copyright Office's AI reports](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/) treat those as separate topics. This license does not assert a universal answer for every jurisdiction or use.

## GitHub hosting is a separate grant

The [GitHub Terms of Service](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service), checked 2026-10-25, grant GitHub and affiliates AI-training rights in D.4 and public viewing/forking rights in D.5. J.3 says the AI-input/output opt-out does not limit the separate public-repository grants.

This repository license cannot override those agreements. If withholding those grants is essential, use a hosting/distribution arrangement with compatible terms and consider access restrictions. These files change no hosting settings.

Sections 3 and 4 of LICENSE expressly refer to section 5. Section 5 makes clear that the license's restrictions, redistribution requirements, and termination clause do not limit rights separately and validly granted by the relevant copyright holders under the applicable GitHub Terms of Service or another hosting agreement. A separate grant keeps its own scope, conditions, and duration. The clarification neither grants additional rights nor creates a blanket exemption for hosts, affiliates, users, models, or forks. This resolves the wording ambiguity; it is not approval from GitHub or a guarantee against account/repository restrictions.

robots.txt and crawler-specific notices are advisory rather than authentication; [RFC 9309](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9309.html) explicitly distinguishes crawler rules from access authorization. A repository file is not control over its hosting platform's root robots.txt. No claim of blocking GitHub crawlers or external models is made here.

An isolated media distribution must carry an accompanying license/notice. Contributions intended for inclusion must be made with authority to grant the project's license for the contributed material. Do not add third-party material under the project's license without permission. Keep separately licensed components identified.
