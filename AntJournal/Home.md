# Ant Journal

Public observations of ant colonies, with original photographs and dated field notes.

[Browse the observation archive and calendar](Archive.md). Open an entry to read the keeper's observations and view its photographs. The archive uses ordinary Markdown and can be read without Obsidian.

| Colony | Species | Pages |

Dates are local observation dates. Estimates retain `~`; unknown values retain `?`. Missing days are unobserved. No synthetic records are included in this journal.
